Why are you receiving this notification?

This is to notify you that a lender has reviewed your CIBIL Rank and Company
Credit Report (CCR).

Whenever you submit an application for a loan or credit card, the lender may check your CIBIL Rank and Company Credit Report, which will be noted as "Enquiries" in your report.

Learn the key terms related to enquiries mentioned in this notification.

WON (Work Order Number)

A unique 9–10-digit number generated when the lender checks your CIBIL Rank.

Enquiry Purpose

The type of credit requested like commercial loan.

Enquiry Date & Time

When the lender requested your credit history and CIBIL Rank.
